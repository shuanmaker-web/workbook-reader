@group 四、閱讀測驗

@passage
Spirited Away is the most famous feature-length animated film by Japanese manga artist, animator, and director Hayao Miyazaki. The main inspiration for Spirited Away came when Miyazaki spent a vacation in a mountain cabin with his family and five 10-year-old girls who were friends of the family. He noticed that the manga the girls were reading contained subjects mainly related to crushes and romance. These were not themes he believed girls at that age really cared about, so Miyazaki set out to create a heroine who all young girls could look up to. Thus, Sen is not portrayed as a typically cute girl like the leading female characters of most anime, and she emerges from most of her struggles through hard work and by building relationships with others. Her character develops by evaluating her own past experiences, not by simply "growing up" to be like her parents. Also typical of Miyazaki's films is the lack of a clear villain. The witch Yubaba and the monstrous No-Face cause a great deal of trouble for Sen, but later demonstrate good reasons for their actions.

Much of the story takes place within a mysterious bathhouse filled with ghosts and spirits. Although Miyazaki opted to limit the use of computer-generated effects and present simpler hand-drawn animated visuals, the animation nonetheless shows incredible detail in its depiction of scenery. Besides similarities to Japan's Dogo Onsen, the buildings outside the bathhouse are visibly inspired by Taiwan's old gold town of Jiufen. Thanks to its interesting story and beautiful visuals, Spirited Away remains one of the most beloved animated films of all time.
@end

@group 五、混合題

@passage
There is more to coral reefs than meets the eye. Many people think that coral is a type of plant or rock, but they are actually animals. So do you know what corals feed on? Most corals rely on algae, which feed them and give them their color. They also eat tiny fish that they catch. Corals are classified as either soft or hard. The hard ones produce a rock-like outer shell and form a coral reef as they attach themselves to rocks and to each other. The largest of these reefs is the Great Barrier Reef in Australia, which is more than 2,300 kilometers long. Many species of fish, such as clownfish and sharks, can be found there. In fact, around a quarter of all ocean species depend on coral for food and shelter. Unfortunately, corals are under serious threat.

Most people believe that if nothing is done to protect them, all live coral reefs could disappear by 2050. What poses the biggest threat to coral reefs is rising water temperatures. The warming of the ocean causes corals to become stressed, to drive out the algae that live inside them, and to turn white. Even if this doesn't kill the corals completely, it does weaken them to the point of dying. Polluted waters, fishing, and taking coral from the ocean also harms reefs.

If you ask how we can protect coral reefs, well, one step that is already being taken is to make them protected areas. We can also help by being more environmentally friendly. Coral reefs play an important part in preserving ocean life. If we don't act now to protect them, our oceans will be a lot less colorful.
@end
