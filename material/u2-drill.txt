Q 1 | fill | 試題演練
stem: The air f______ter in the lab removes harmful particles effectively, so the scientists can work safely without fear of contamination.
= filter
~! air filter 空氣過濾器，能有效去除有害微粒（particles），故填 filter。

Q 2 | fill | 試題演練
stem: The company always adjusts its strategies to suit the changing c______ces of the market, and this flexibility helps it stay competitive.
= circumstances
~! the changing circumstances of the market 市場不斷變化的情況；the 後接複數名詞，故為 circumstances。

Q 3 | fill | 試題演練
stem: Sandpaper feels c______se to the touch and is most often used by carpenters for smoothing rough wood surfaces.
= coarse
~! 砂紙用來磨平粗糙的木頭表面，摸起來是「粗糙的」，故填 coarse。

Q 4 | fill | 試題演練
stem: The students asked the teacher to c______y the confusing problem so that they could understand it better.
= clarify
~! 問題令人困惑，學生請老師把它「釐清」，才能更理解，故填 clarify（本字不在本單元單字表中）。

Q 5 | bank | 試題演練
stem: The cat ______ the furniture whenever it feels nervous, and its owner tries to calm it down by giving it toys to play with.
= F
~! 主詞 The cat 為第三人稱單數，whenever 子句用現在式，故填 scratches（抓、刮傢俱）。

Q 6 | bank | 試題演練
stem: The stars were ______ after the clouds cleared from the night sky, and everyone was amazed by the beautiful sight.
= A
~! 雲散去後星星變得「看得見」，故選 visible。

Q 7 | bank | 試題演練
stem: The mechanic performed a thorough ______ of the car before approving it for long-distance journeys. The driver appreciated his careful work.
= D
~! a thorough inspection 徹底的檢查；技師核准車輛長途行駛前做的是檢查。

Q 8 | bank | 試題演練
stem: To ______ the task within the deadline, the team worked tirelessly, collaborating effectively to overcome every obstacle.
= E
~! To + 原形動詞表目的；accomplish the task within the deadline 在期限內完成任務。

Q 9 | bank | 試題演練
stem: The floor has just been cleaned and is still ______, so there is a sign to warn people to walk carefully.
= G
~! 地板剛清潔完還是濕的，立牌提醒小心走，故選 slippery（濕滑的）。

@group 學測詞彙題

Q 10 | choice | 學測詞彙題
stem: Nouns like "apple" and "book" are ______ and so they have a common plural form. The students are practicing these nouns with numbers.
A: indifferent
B: countable
C: minimal
D: agreeable
= B
~! apple、book 是可以用數字計數、有複數形的名詞，即可數名詞 countable nouns。

Q 11 | choice | 學測詞彙題
stem: The ______ of the meeting was supposed to be an hour, but it had to be extended for additional discussion because we still couldn't reach an agreement.
A: duration
B: tribute
C: combination
D: dispute
= A
~! 會議原本預計一小時，指會議的「持續時間」，故選 duration。

Q 12 | choice | 學測詞彙題
stem: The technician carefully ______ the samples before placing them in storage so that they could be easily identified during later analysis.
A: assured
B: labeled
C: objected
D: attributed
= B
~! 為了日後分析時容易辨識，存放前先在樣本上「貼標籤」，故選 labeled。

Q 13 | choice | 學測詞彙題
stem: Brian felt great ______ after working every day for a month, so he decided to take a phone-free vacation to recover.
A: essence
B: fatigue
C: compassion
D: insurance
= B
~! 連續工作一個月後感到極度疲勞，才決定度假恢復，故選 fatigue。

Q 14 | choice | 學測詞彙題
stem: Water is ______ for human survival, playing a vital role in maintaining body functions and overall health in daily life.
A: invisible
B: muscular
C: essential
D: global
= C
~! be essential for N. 對…是必要的；水對人類生存扮演關鍵角色。
