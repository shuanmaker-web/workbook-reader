@group 一、綜合測驗

@passage
It was the final year of the twentieth century. A Columbia University professor and his students were coming up with a smart food-growing solution. [[1]] planting crops in traditional horizontal ground-level rows, they proposed stacking trays of edible greens and herbs on top of one another.

These tasty towers of produce could be constructed and housed in controlled environments. This would [[2]] them largely free of bugs and bacteria, and artificial LED lighting could be employed to ensure the crops received adequate UV rays to grow. Moreover, the vertical stacks would maximize land usage and enable highly efficient water recycling and irrigation processes.

Putting all these innovative agricultural elements together, the team eventually designed a stunning high-tech skyscraper of a farm. In theory, it would reach several stories into the air, [[3]] sustainably feeding up to nearly 50,000 people. However, even after more than twenty years since it was conceived, this grand vision has yet to be realized. [[4]] vertical farming isn't reaching great heights, though. Millions of dollars in venture capital and private investment are currently pouring in to this cutting-edge form of agriculture. In addition, it's already [[5]] in North America and the Middle East, and vertical crop-maximizing technologies and techniques are being refined daily.

All in all, vertical farming promises a bright future, not only in agricultural production but also in sustainable practices.
@end

@passage
There are three key components required to create a functional touch screen. These are the touch sensor, the software, and the "controller" that facilitates communication between the two.

First of all, the touch sensor, which comes in three different types, is responsible for detecting a touch "event." Most such sensors work by utilizing two metal layers separated by a tiny gap. When a user touches a specific spot on the screen, this will apply pressure on that area's surface, causing the layers to [[6]] and close the gap. This, in turn, brings about certain changes in electric current or charge, or sound waves, [[7]] signaling the nature of the event.

It's then up to the middleman, the intervening controller component, to interpret these signals and determine exactly which sensors [[8]]. Once it has established the precise location of the "touch," it can pass on the relevant information to the software. This third and final touch screen element will then ensure that the event is matched by an [[9]] computing response.

Miraculously, all of this signaling and relaying of information takes place within a [[10]] of a second. In practical terms, the software's reaction to a specific touch is virtually instantaneous, owing to the efficient coordination among these components.
@end

@passage
Technology involves applying scientific knowledge in order to create or improve upon advanced machines or equipment. This is precisely what Willis Carrier did in 1902, when he [[11]] a mechanical invention that would fundamentally transform the way people experienced summer. His innovative air conditioning system ended up changing far more than just the season, however—it changed the world.

Carrier's eureka [[12]] came on a humble train platform. One day, while standing on one of these everyday surfaces and staring into the fog, he suddenly [[13]] upon a brilliant idea. Why not have warm, humid air pass over a liquid cooling agent that could absorb heat and moisture, and turn the liquid into a gas in the process?

All one would have to do then was to separate the cooled air from this newly formed [[14]], redirecting the former into the space that required cooling, and the latter into a coil or chamber where the heat could be released. Over time, the temperature of the cooling agent would drop enough to return it to a liquid state, allowing it to be reused [[15]] to extract heat from more warm air.

For the first time, Carrier's [[16]] refrigeration cycle allowed us to create cooler, more comfortable indoor environments at will, all year round. As a result, large-scale food production [[17]] as items such as produce and meat could be preserved more easily. In addition, an artificially cooled indoor cinema was suddenly the perfect [[18]] from the sweltering summer heat outside. Before long, the new technology was being used to render long-distance passenger flights [[19]], to cool computer servers that were constantly calculating, and to build huge shopping malls for the masses.

Ever since, Carrier's brilliant innovation has been cooling and drying our homes, hospitals, offices, schools, factories, vehicles, and [[20]] every other indoor space imaginable. It is utterly impossible to imagine modern life without the ubiquitous benefit of air conditioning.
@end

@passage
Dogs can be a handful, so much so that their two-legged owners are often compelled to turn to external service providers for assistance. [[21]] These are services from costly pet care professionals such as groomers, trainers, and walkers abound. Chances are, even the odd "dog whisperer," being an expert pet communicator, is just a phone call away too.

[[22]] Instead, what's needed to ease the burden of domestic animal ownership is simply some intelligent and compatible manmade machinery. One way technology is making dog lovers' lives easier is during playtime. iFetch is a patented device that can keep your best furry friend busy and active when you have better things to do. [[23]] Dogs can drop retrieved balls down into this spacious tunnel, after which the iFetch will then automatically launch the ball out through an opening at its opposite end.

Depending on which model you have, the mechanical playmate can be set to "throw" the ball through either a fixed, or a random opening. The force with which the bouncing missile is expelled from the portable, fully rechargeable little cannon can also be adjusted. [[24]] What'll be even sweeter music to dog owners' ears is that the iFetch isn't noisy at all and is completely safe.

With technology lending a helping hand, the question emerges—who truly is a person's best friend? Is it the playful pup scrambling across the carpet to hunt down that moving ball, or is it the time-saving technology that throw balls on behalf of a dog-tired owner?
@end
