= 段落文意連貫性與一致性
通常每個文章段落只會針對一個主題重點討論。段落中文意會相互連貫，主題論述會保持一致。
+例 Solar panels have gained widespread popularity due to their environmental benefits. They produce clean energy by harnessing sunlight, which reduces greenhouse gas emissions and dependence on fossil fuels. Additionally, solar power is a renewable resource, meaning it won't run out as long as the sun continues to shine. This makes solar panels a sustainable option for generating electricity for homes and businesses. | 第一段文意一致陳述優點 benefits；關鍵字：produce clean energy、renewable resource、sustainable option。
+例 However, despite their advantages, solar panels come with some limitations. For example, they can be expensive to install, and their efficiency is heavily dependent on weather conditions. On cloudy days, the amount of energy generated can decrease significantly. Another issue is the amount of space required for optimal energy capture, which can be a challenge in urban areas. | 第二段從 However 語意轉折後，文意皆為陳述缺點 limitations；關鍵字：expensive、dependent on weather conditions、space required。

@passage
Since the early 1990s, the lithium-ion battery has been the most suitable battery for portable electronic equipment. Today, they're commonly used for cellphones, computers, tablets, digital cameras, and other devices.

Lithium-ion batteries have nearly twice the energy density of traditional nickel cadmium batteries. [[1]] This feature has important implications for cellphones and computers, because it makes these items more portable for consumers. It also makes power tools easier to use and allows workers to use them for longer periods of time.

The lithium-ion battery is also a low maintenance battery. Lithium-ion batteries retain no "memory" of their power capacity from previous charging cycles. Thus they require no scheduled cycling and can be fully re-fueled to their maximum capacity during each charging cycle. Other rechargeable battery types, in contrast, retain information from previous charging cycles, which wastes valuable storage space. Over time, this makes these rechargeable batteries hold less of a charge.

[[2]] It is fragile and requires a protection circuit to maintain safe operation. A high load could overheat the pack and safety might be jeopardized. [[3]] After 2-3 years of use, the pack often becomes unserviceable due to a large voltage drop caused by high internal resistance.

It should be noted, however, that manufacturers are constantly making improvements on lithium-ion batteries. [[4]] With such rapid progress, the use of lithium-ion batteries will certainly expand further.

@passage
Wind power is a technology that converts wind energy into electricity. Its basic principle involves using the force of the wind to drive the rotation of wind turbine blades. [[5]] Wind farms are usually constructed in areas with high wind speeds, and the availability of wind resources and economic viability are important considerations.

In fact, more and more companies are joining the RE100 initiative, committing to sourcing 100% of their electricity from renewable sources. On the list are prominent names like Apple, Google, Microsoft, Hon Hai and TSMC. [[6]]

In addition to voluntary commitments by businesses, many countries are actively promoting the development of wind power. For instance, numerous European countries have set renewable energy targets and provide corresponding policy support and incentives. [[7]] One of their goals is to address growing energy demand and reduce reliance on fossil fuels.

Despite its many advantages, there are still some who oppose wind power due to potential health effects of wind turbines on residents. [[8]] Moreover, there are concerns about the environmental impact of wind power, such as the effects of constructing wind turbines on land and water resources.

Overall, while wind power is an important clean energy source, it still requires careful consideration and balancing of various interests to ensure its sustainability and environmental friendliness.
@end
