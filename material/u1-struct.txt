@group 學測重點：代名詞
@page 16
= 代名詞的用法
文章中的代名詞，常用以代替、呼應前文提及的人事物，或前方整個文意。
> 常見代名詞：he、she、it、they、him、her、them、this、that、these、those、myself、yourself、himself、herself、ourselves、themselves、itself
+例 Even though John was nervous about the competition, he still gave it his best shot. | he 為前句 John 的代名詞；it 則是前句指的 competition。
+例 Emily sewed her own dress for the event. Her design was very unique. | Her design 為前句 her own dress 的代名詞（Emily 創作的服裝）。
+例 The government passed a new law to protect endangered species. This was widely praised by environmentalists. | This 為前句整個文意的代名詞。
+例 The director used bold lighting and dramatic music to heighten the tension in the film. These intense elements made the climax unforgettable. | These intense elements 意指前句的 bold lighting 和 dramatic music。

@group 歷屆試題範例
@bank
A: Scientists call this unidirectional regeneration.
B: The same thing happens for lobsters, salamanders, and many other animals.
C: When it comes to regeneration, few animals can equal the magic of the planarian.
D: They hope that this line of research will make regeneration possible in humans someday.
E: Humans aren't completely without regenerative talents.

@passage
One of the most difficult things for a human to face is the loss of a limb. If a person loses an arm or a leg, he/she must be fitted with an artificial limb.

The situation is very different for a starfish. If a starfish loses an arm, it can grow a new one. [[1]] Snails can even regrow their heads—imagine what the world would be like if humans could do that. But we can't. Nor can we grow new limbs or even fingers. That's why scientists are studying animals that can regrow body parts, that is, regenerate. [[2]]

Many different kinds of animals show some form of regeneration. Most of them are, however, limited to the sort a lizard is capable of, like regrowing a lost tail. A cockroach can grow back a missing limb, but the limb itself can't generate a new cockroach. [[3]] Bidirectional regeneration, on the other hand, refers to a situation in which splitting of an animal will result in separate fully functional animals. This type of regeneration is demonstrated in a few animals, such as hydras and sea stars. Cut a hydra in half, and you'll get two hydras. Cut it into four pieces, and you'll get four. [[4]] A single one can be cut into hundreds of pieces and each will grow back into a whole new worm in a week or so. Because of this remarkable ability, one planarian can be created over and over, giving it a sort of immortality. Whether this phenomenon can be achieved in humans will likely require years of research.
@end

Q 1 | bank | 106指考
stem: （第 1 格）請由方框中選出最適合的句子。
= B
~ The same thing 即意指前句 If a starfish loses an arm, it can grow a new one.；依文意可推斷答案為選項 (B)。
~文意 對海星來說，情況大不相同。如果海星失去一隻手臂，牠能長出一隻新的手臂。(B) 同樣的事也發生在龍蝦、蠑螈和許多其他動物。蝸牛連頭都能長回——想像假如人類能這樣，世界會變什麼樣子。

Q 2 | bank | 106指考
stem: （第 2 格）請由方框中選出最適合的句子。
= D
~ this line of research 即意指前句 that is, regenerate.；依文意可推斷答案為選項 (D)。
~文意 這就是為什麼科學家正研究能重新長出身體部位（也就是再生）的動物。(D) 他們希望這項研究將使人類有一天能再生。

= A
~ 句中的 this 即意指前句 A cockroach can grow back a missing limb, but the limb itself can't generate a new cockroach.；依文意可推斷答案為選項 (A)。

Q 4 | bank | 106指考
stem: （第 4 格）請由方框中選出最適合的句子。
= C
~ 後句 A single one 可知為前句中某人事物的代名詞。依空格後句往前推斷文意，可知答案為選項 (C)；one 即是選項 (C) 中 planarian（渦蟲）的代名詞。*選項 (E) 人類並非完全沒有再生能力。
~文意 (C) 當提到再生，幾乎沒有動物能和渦蟲的能力相比。單一隻渦蟲可以切成數百段，約一週後，每段都會長成完整個體。

@passage
School bullying is a significant issue across Asia. In Japan, for instance, a government survey in 2019 indicated over 600,000 cases of school bullying, highlighting its widespread nature. South Korea also faces severe challenges, with reports of bullying leading to tragic consequences, including student suicides. China and India, with their vast populations, encounter similar issues. [[5]]

Several factors contribute to the prevalence of school bullying in Asia. Cultural norms and social pressures play a significant role. In many Asian cultures, maintaining family honor and high academic performance can lead to immense pressure on students. [[6]] Moreover, a lack of awareness and inadequate anti-bullying policies in schools contribute to the issue's persistence. In some cases, teachers and administrators may overlook or underestimate bullying. [[7]]

Addressing school bullying in Asia requires a comprehensive approach involving students, parents, educators, and policymakers. Firstly, increasing awareness through education campaigns can help communities understand the severity of bullying and its long-term impacts on mental health. [[8]] Providing support services, such as counseling and peer support groups, can help victims recover and build resilience. By taking these steps, Asian countries can create safer and more inclusive school environments, reducing the prevalence and impact of bullying.
@end
