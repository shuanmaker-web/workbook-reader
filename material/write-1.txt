@section wr-1 | 寫作（一）：表個人看法／意見／經驗分享 | Writing: opinions & experiences | 學測重點：主題寫作
@overview 此題型屬於夾敘述夾議論的類型。1. 重點在於表達個人意見或分享經驗，並帶出原因與支持句。2. 描述原因細節時，用不同角度切入，找出不同論點，增加文章內容全面性。3. 第二段常要求撰寫為了達成特定目的的做法。

@group 歷屆寫作範例

Q 1 | write | 110指考
stem: 提示：近年來，很多大學鼓勵教授以英語講授專業課程，請寫一篇英文作文，說明你對這個現象的看法。文分兩段，第一段說明你是否認同這個趨勢並陳述理由；第二段說明如果你未來就讀的大學必修課是以英語授課，你將會如何因應或規劃。
= From my perspective, it is a positive trend for universities to offer courses in English. Firstly, English is the global language of communication, and it is important for students to be proficient in it. By learning in English, students are enabled to develop stronger language skills, which will benefit their future careers. Moreover, many academic papers and resources are written in English. By acquiring English proficiency, students can access a wider range of knowledge, improving their learning outcomes. What's more, English instruction prepares students for international opportunities, such as studying abroad or working for global companies. Overall, universities adopting English as the medium of instruction can greatly benefit students. ¶ If I were required to take courses in English, I recognize the importance of adjusting my study habits. To begin with, I will familiarize myself with key vocabulary before classes to enhance my comprehension. Besides, I will take detailed notes and review them regularly. What's more, I will listen to English podcasts to improve my listening ability, as it is important that I understand lectures clearly. Lastly, I will actively participate in discussions to practice expressing my thoughts in English. Through executing these strategies, I believe I can adapt to an English-based learning environment and succeed.
~ 範文照錄書上「歷屆寫作範例」。第一段用 From my perspective 表明看法，再以 Firstly / Moreover / What's more / Overall 串起理由；第二段用 recognize the importance of、To begin with / Besides / What's more / Lastly 列出做法，以 Through executing these strategies 收尾。

Q 2 | write | 試題演練
stem: 說明：依提示寫一篇英文作文，文長至少 120 個單詞（words）。
  提示：許多歐美的高中或是大學生，在畢業之後並不會馬上進入職場或是繼續升學，而是利用一年的時間，來進行一些自我實現的計畫，如擔任志工／旅遊／學習語言等，當作是充實自己的機會，稱為 gap year。請同學寫一篇兩段的文章，第一段討論您是否認同這樣的趨勢，第二段則分享若您有一年的 gap year，您打算做怎樣的安排，並說明原因。
= From my perspective, taking a gap year is a meaningful trend that more students in Taiwan should consider. Firstly, after years of preparing for exams, many students have never had the chance to think about what they truly want. A gap year enables them to explore their interests before choosing a major or a career. Moreover, by volunteering, traveling, or learning a new language, students gain real-world experience that cannot be found in textbooks. What's more, living on their own for a year helps them become more independent and responsible. Overall, as long as it is carefully planned, a gap year can be a valuable investment in one's future. ¶ If I had a gap year, I would spend it on two goals. To begin with, I would work as a volunteer English teacher in a remote village for six months. Through teaching children, I could not only improve my communication skills but also find out whether education is the right career for me. Besides, I would use the rest of the year to travel around Japan on a working holiday. To achieve this, I would start learning Japanese and save money in advance. In the long run, I believe these experiences would make me more mature and better prepared for college life.
~! 書上沒有範文，以上範文為本 app 撰寫，僅供參考。第一段用 From my perspective 表明立場，以 Firstly / Moreover / What's more / Overall 列出理由；第二段用 To begin with / Besides / To achieve this / In the long run 說明 gap year 的安排與原因。
