Q 1 | fill | 試題演練
stem: The customer called the hotel to c______m her reservation for the weekend, ensuring her room was booked in advance.
= confirm
~! 打電話給飯店「確認」週末的訂房，確保房間已事先訂好；to + 原形動詞，故填 confirm。

Q 2 | fill | 試題演練
stem: Many people believe that spreading g______p can be harmless fun, but it often causes serious misunderstandings in relationships.
= gossip
~! spread gossip 散布八卦；八卦常造成人際間的誤會，故填 gossip（不可數名詞，不加 s）。

Q 3 | fill | 試題演練
stem: The football player's severe i______y during the intense match required immediate medical attention. Thankfully, he is now recovering very well.
= injury
~! 比賽中嚴重「受傷」需要立即就醫，現在正在康復；severe injury 嚴重受傷，故填 injury。

Q 4 | fill | 試題演練
stem: The novel's protagonist faced a difficult m______l dilemma, forcing her to choose between personal gain and doing the right thing.
= moral
~! 在個人利益與做對的事之間抉擇，是「道德的」兩難；a moral dilemma 道德困境，故填 moral。

Q 6 | bank | 試題演練

= C
~! 起司熟成六個月後達到所需的「成熟度」；the level of + 名詞，故選 maturity。

Q 7 | bank | 試題演練

~! have no choice but to + 原形動詞；叛軍失去基地後只好向政府「投降」，衝突才結束；surrender to N. 向……投降。

Q 8 | bank | 試題演練

= A
~! based on + 名詞；時間有限時憑「直覺」做決定雖有風險卻常是最好的方法，故選 intuition。

Q 10 | choice | 學測詞彙題
stem: Apple Inc. used to ______ the smartphone market in this country; however, Samsung seems to be catching up these days.
A: overlook
B: transport
C: dominate
D: request
= C
~! dominate the market 控制／主宰市場；however 後說三星正在迎頭趕上，可知蘋果過去「主宰」市場。overlook 忽視、transport 運輸、request 請求，皆不合語意。

Q 11 | choice | 學測詞彙題
stem: Ms. Ko provided a list of books as ______ for her students' research projects, and they were very thankful to her because these materials helped them a lot.
A: authority
B: discipline
C: reference
D: ownership
= C
~! 老師提供書單作為研究專題的「參考資料」，學生因這些資料而受益，故選 reference。authority 權威、discipline 紀律、ownership 所有權，皆不合語意。

Q 12 | choice | 學測詞彙題
stem: Often, if someone ______ guilty to a crime or an offence they commit, they will get a less serious punishment.
A: migrates
B: resorts
C: defines
D: pleads
= D
~! plead guilty 認罪；認罪通常可獲較輕的刑罰，故選 pleads。migrate 移居、resort 訴諸、define 定義，皆不合語意。

Q 13 | choice | 學測詞彙題
stem: Her degree and experience give her the ______ needed to apply for the managerial position, and she is confident that her skills will get her the job.
A: residences
B: qualifications
C: conquests
D: landmarks
= B
~! 學歷與經驗讓她具備申請管理職所需的「資格」，故選 qualifications。residence 住宅、conquest 征服、landmark 地標，皆不合語意。

Q 14 | choice | 學測詞彙題
stem: Paul has been hired in a ______ position that will only last three months, and once the project is finished he will be out of work again.
A: legitimate
B: temporary
C: concrete
D: crispy
= B
~! 只持續三個月、專案結束後又會失業，是「臨時的」職位，故選 temporary。legitimate 合法的、concrete 具體的、crispy 酥脆的，皆不合語意。
